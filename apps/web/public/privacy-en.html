Privacy Notice

This page is a translation. The Korean notice is the governing version.

Yeochana has no sign-up or login, and it doesn't collect personal information such as your name or contact details.

Your Saved places stay on this device

Your Saved places and their dates, places you removed with “Been there,” your stay (including a place picked with the “Address or stay” search), the destination you picked (Jeju, Busan, Gangwon, Seoul or somewhere else) and, for Gangwon, Seoul and somewhere else, the last district (si/gun/gu), the area and range you picked, and the language you picked are kept only in this device's browser storage. The only things sent to a server are the neighborhood name for “rain alerts” (below; a district's grid cell for Gangwon, Seoul and somewhere else), the words you search under “Address or stay” and the address of the place you pick (below), what's listed under “Usage stats,” and any feedback you send yourself with “Send feedback.” The place files for somewhere else and the “Open in browser” address are covered under “Your location” below.

If you clear your browser data, these are cleared too. “Back up as link” puts your Saved list after the # in the link's address. The part after the # is never sent to a server.

Your location never leaves the device

Your location is used only on this device, to pick nearby places, estimate travel times, and tell whether you're in Jeju, Busan, Gangwon (Gangneung, Sokcho, Chuncheon, Yangyang, Pyeongchang), Seoul (Jongno, Jung, Yongsan, Seongdong, Gwangjin, Dongdaemun, Jungnang, Seongbuk, Gangbuk, Dobong, Nowon, Eunpyeong, Seodaemun, Mapo, Yangcheon, Gangseo, Guro, Geumcheon, Yeongdeungpo, Dongjak, Gwanak, Seocho, Gangnam, Songpa and Gangdong districts) or which other district (si/gun/gu). The first time you open the app, a start screen appears; tapping “Start with my location” there asks for your location. While your basis is your location, it is also asked for when you tap something that needs it, such as a mood, “Where I am now” or “My location” in the basis sheet, or your saved places. If you have already allowed location access, it is read without asking when the app opens or when you return to it. Coordinates are never sent off the device. Links to outside map apps (Naver Map, KakaoMap, TMAP) contain only the name and coordinates of where you're going (for an island, the ferry terminal) and the KakaoMap place ID. Your current location is not included. The map app sets the starting point on that device.

Places for somewhere else come in one file per province or metropolitan city (si/do): the file of the one your current district is in, and those of up to two nearby ones. Like any other file request, the address of that request (nation/<province number>.json) may be kept in Cloudflare's access logs. It contains no coordinates and no district. When you use “Open in browser” from an in-app browser such as KakaoTalk's, the address handed over includes, for Gangwon, Seoul and somewhere else, the number of the district in use (area=), even when it was set by your location. Wherever you use the app, it also includes the destination in use (dest=, e.g. dest=gangwon for Gangwon). Opening that address may also be kept in Cloudflare's access logs, and Naver Analytics may receive the address as it is (GA4 gets it without the part after the ?).

Booking links on experience cards go to Naver Booking, MyRealTrip, or the business's own website, or place a phone call (your phone app opens). These links contain only that business's address or phone number. Your location and this app's records are not included. Any booking and personal information after that are governed by that site's or business's own policy.

The map app you choose at the bottom of the Saved screen is stored only on this device. If it isn't Naver Map, its name is also included in “Back up as link.”

Rain alerts

If you've picked an area or a stay, or checked your location this time, the app asks this site's server whether rain is expected in the next 3 hours. It sends just one neighborhood name. Your current location is converted on the device to the nearest neighborhood, and coordinates are not sent. The server gets the Korea Meteorological Administration's short-range forecast for that neighborhood and returns only the rain alert. In Gangwon (Gangneung, Sokcho, Chuncheon, Yangyang, Pyeongchang), Seoul (Jongno, Jung, Yongsan, Seongdong, Gwangjin, Dongdaemun, Jungnang, Seongbuk, Gangbuk, Dobong, Nowon, Eunpyeong, Seodaemun, Mapo, Yangcheon, Gangseo, Guro, Geumcheon, Yeongdeungpo, Dongjak, Gwanak, Seocho, Gangnam, Songpa and Gangdong districts) and anywhere else outside Jeju and Busan, it sends one Korea Meteorological Administration grid cell number for the center of that district (not your current location) instead of a neighborhood name. Like any other visit, this request may show up in Cloudflare's access logs.

Happening nearby today

When your destination is Seoul and a starting point is set, the home asks this site's server once a day for the whole list of Seoul cultural events. No location, coordinates or neighbourhood name is sent. The server gets the Seoul cultural event information from Seoul Open Data Plaza and returns only what is on today; which ones are near is worked out on this device. Show information comes to the server from KOPIS (the Korea Performing Arts Box Office Information System of the Korea Arts Management Service) and is returned with it; no location is sent to KOPIS either. Like any other visit, this request may appear in Cloudflare's access logs.

When you open “Browse Seoul today” on the Seoul home, show posters come through this site (yeochana.com), made smaller. This site's Cloudflare fetches the originals from KOPIS (kopis.or.kr), resizes them and keeps them in its cache for a while, so KOPIS never sees this device's IP address. Pictures from the Seoul cultural events site are not loaded. Only pictures about to come on screen are loaded, and no location is sent. Like other requests, these may be kept in Cloudflare's access logs (the address holds only the picture's address).

Address or stay search

When you press Search (or Enter) under “Address or stay,” the words in the box are sent to this site's server, which asks Kakao (the Kakao Local API) for matching addresses and places and returns only their names, addresses and coordinates. Nothing is sent as you type, and your current location is never sent. The words go in the body of the request, not in its address, so if this request shows up in Cloudflare's access logs like any other visit, the address recorded there (/api/geo) doesn't contain what you searched. The server stores neither your search nor its results, and doesn't keep them in Cloudflare's cache either. The addresses and coordinates from Kakao are used only to show the results and, if no official address is found, as your stay in the page you have open for this visit; they're never saved on this device. When you pick one of the results, its address (or its name, if it has no address) is sent the same way, in the body of the request, to the server (/api/addr), which asks the Ministry of the Interior and Safety's road name address API (business.juso.go.kr) for the official address and coordinates and returns them. These aren't stored or cached on the server either. What's saved only on this device, as your stay, are these official coordinates. If no official address is found, the place you picked is used as your stay for this visit only and isn't saved. Reloading or reopening the page goes back to the starting point you saved before. To prevent too many requests, the server counts them per IP address over one minute in its memory only and doesn't store them. Kakao and the Ministry handle the words they receive under their own policies.

Usage stats

To see which features get used, usage stats are recorded in three places: this site's anonymous stats, Google Analytics (GA4), and Naver Analytics. At the bottom of the Saved screen there's a line that says “We record how you use the app (including Google and Naver visit stats).” and a “Turn off stats” button.

What's sent to this site's anonymous stats:

  • Place ID and action (shown as a card, saved, unsaved, outside map app link opened, booking link opened, “Been there” tapped and undone, mood tapped)
  • The mood and subtype you picked (meal, café, etc.), range, type of starting point (the area name, if you picked an area; in Gangwon, Seoul or somewhere else, the district you picked), whether you picked another day, whether “Indoor only” was on, and the season shown on the card (cherry blossom season, etc.)
  • Whether you opened your Saved list, how many days later you opened a Saved place (number of days only), and whether you tidied up the list
  • The destination in use (Jeju, Busan, Gangwon, Seoul or somewhere else)
  • Whether the start screen appeared the first time you opened the app, whether you started there with your location or chose a destination yourself (if you chose, why your location couldn't be used before that: denied, not found, or outside the destinations), and the destination you picked
  • If you arrived through a stay's link, that link's tag (arrivals in that visit, mood taps, and outside map app link opens are counted separately for each tag)
  • Whether you opened “Browse Seoul today”, how long it stayed open (in 5-second steps), how many rows you scrolled sideways, how many cards you tapped (0, 1, or 2 or more), and whether you tapped Directions or Details on a card (never which event or show, and no location)
  • Date (no time of day)

This site's server (Cloudflare) only keeps counts per date, place, and action. It neither reads nor stores IP addresses, browser information, or cookies.

GA4 receives page views, mood taps, shown as a card, outside map app link opened, saved, opening and tidying up the Saved list, and the start screen appearing and being used to start. These carry only the place ID, the mood and subtype you picked, range, type of starting point, whether you picked another day, the season shown on the card, whether it was opened from the Saved list, how many days since it was saved (number of days only) and whether it was in a past-day row, whether there were no saves for today or later and whether there was a past-days group when the list was opened, the type of tidy-up, the destination in use (Jeju, Busan, Gangwon, Seoul or somewhere else), and whether you started on the start screen with your location or chose yourself, with why your location couldn't be used before that. Naver Analytics receives page views only. Both services use cookies and process connection information such as IP addresses and browser information on their own servers. They may also use cookies to count visits from the same browser as connected. That processing is governed by Google's and Naver's privacy policies. Google signals and ad personalization are turned off in GA4.

Coordinates are never sent to any of these three. The records this app creates contain no time of day or device ID.

Tapping “Turn off stats” stops them right away and discards any records not yet sent. All three stop. If you open the app with stats off, the GA4 and Naver Analytics scripts aren't loaded at all. Stats stay off until you turn them back on. You can delete cookies that were already set in your browser settings.

Send feedback

You can send feedback with “Send feedback” at the very bottom of the Saved screen, or with “How was it? 1-minute survey” at the very bottom of the home screen. You don't have to answer everything, and nothing is sent until you tap “Send.”

What's sent is the answers you picked (whether you went to the places suggested, what was inconvenient), anything you wrote, and the app version. This site's server (Cloudflare) adds only the date (no time of day) and stores it. It does not store IP addresses, browser information, cookies, location, place IDs, or device IDs. Please don't include your name or contact details in what you write. If more than 200 come in on one day, any after that aren't stored; only how many there were is counted.

Feedback is deleted after one year. Because there's no way to tell who sent what, we can't find and delete only your feedback.

The survey line on the home screen appears only when you reopen the app after opening an outside map app link once, and no more than three times. Once you tap it, it won't appear again. To do this, whether you've opened a map link and how many times the line has appeared are recorded on this device only. If stats are turned off, the survey line doesn't appear. “Send feedback” works whether stats are on or off.

Access logs

The hosting that serves this site (Cloudflare) and the servers that deliver place photos (Visit Jeju for Jeju, the Korea Tourism Organization for Busan and everywhere else) may keep logs such as IP addresses, like any website. This app doesn't collect those logs separately.
